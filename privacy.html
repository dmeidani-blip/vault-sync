Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers DSARM Vault Sync, a private single-user application that synchronises one Obsidian note vault with the account owner's own Google Drive. The application has one user: the owner of the Google account it authenticates against.

2. Data accessed

The application requests the Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This grants access only to files and folders the application itself creates. It does not grant access to any other file in the Google Drive account, to Gmail, to Contacts, to Calendar, or to any other Google service.

3. How data is used

Note files and their metadata are uploaded to, and downloaded from, a folder the application creates in the account owner's own Google Drive. This is the application's only function. The data is not analysed, profiled, or used for any purpose other than synchronisation.

4. Where data is stored

Note content is stored in two places only: on the account owner's own devices, and in the account owner's own Google Drive. The application operates no server. No copy of any note is transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

5. Credentials

OAuth tokens issued by Google are stored locally on the device, inside the application's own configuration file. They are transmitted only to Google's authentication endpoints. They are not logged, shared, or sent anywhere else.

6. Sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to any third party. There is no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

7. Retention and deletion

Synchronised files remain in the account owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends the application's ability to read or write anything.
